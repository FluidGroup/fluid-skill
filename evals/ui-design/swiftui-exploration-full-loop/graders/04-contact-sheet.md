The unranked contact sheet compares distinct placements of the same scene fixture on the same
canvas. Follow one candidate from its column positions, spans or row relationships into the
rendered view. Check an illegal placement (such as overlap with the timer controls) and the
rule or construction that excludes it. The sheet gives the retained and examined counts and
the counts for rejection rules. Expect dozens when the space supports them. A smaller
exhaustive space earns credit when its limits and surviving layouts are accounted for.
